Bharatam

Bharatam is a gamified digital heritage platform designed to teach Indian civilization, history, and culture through interactive gameplay, storytelling, and AI-driven content — moving away from textbook-based, memorisation-heavy learning that fails to build lasting engagement with heritage among young learners.

Overview

The platform offers two portals:

Individual Portal — built around five domains, with students progressing through an XP, coin, and badge-based reward system, supported by interactive world maps and a central Knowledge Vault.
Student–Teacher Portal — offers NCERT-aligned content, letting teachers assign lessons, games, and modules, and track student progress as part of regular classroom activity.

Alignment with Government & Cultural Initiatives

Bharatam is closely aligned with several government education and cultural initiatives:

NEP 2020 — reflects its emphasis on experiential, activity-based, and technology-enabled learning, including gamification of Indian art and culture
NCERT — the teacher portal directly supports curriculum-linked classroom adoption
Indian Knowledge Systems (IKS) & Bharatiya Khel — advanced by the individual portal through traditional knowledge and games in a modern format
PRERANA Programme (Ministry of Education) — mirrored by the platform's design
UNESCO — contributes to the goal of preserving intangible cultural heritage by keeping traditional games, stories, and practices relevant to younger generations
